## Caveats

- The test set is small, so the bootstrap intervals are wide; rare classes (ambiguous, other_aid) have only a handful of test images and their per-class F1 can swing a lot with one prediction.
- Seed spread only reflects training randomness on one fixed split, not split-to-split variance.
- Splits are grouped by Flickr uploader/creator; one uploader contributes a large share of images and sits entirely in one split.
- Most labels are single-annotator; see `results/manifest_v2_summary.json` for label resolution counts.
